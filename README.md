### 🚀 About Me

I'm a PhD researcher in Meteorology at the National Institute for Space Research (INPE), Brazil. My work focuses on GNSS-derived precipitable water vapor (PWV), precipitation nowcasting, deep learning and tropical convection.

🔭 &nbsp;I'm currently working on **GNSS-PWV-based precipitation nowcasting using machine learning, satellite observations, and atmospheric time series.**  
🌱 &nbsp;I'm currently learning **Advanced deep learning, numerical weather prediction with WRF, and AI applications in atmospheric sciences.**  
👯 &nbsp;I'm looking to collaborate on **Research on GNSS meteorology, tropical convection, satellite remote sensing, and AI for weather forecasting.**  
💬 &nbsp;Ask me about **GNSS meteorology, precipitable water vapor, precipitation nowcasting, Python, and scientific data analysis.**
